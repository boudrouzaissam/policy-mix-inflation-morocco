# Policy Mix and Inflation Dynamics in Morocco

This repository contains the empirical material for a research project on the dynamic relationship between monetary-fiscal policy mix and inflation in Morocco.

## Research objective

The project investigates whether the interaction between monetary and fiscal policy contributes to inflation dynamics, particularly during historically active policy-mix episodes.

## Empirical strategy

The analysis relies on Local Projections estimated over the period 1967–2000. Several definitions of active policy-mix periods are considered to assess the robustness of the results.

## Main finding

The results suggest that the inflationary effect of the monetary-fiscal interaction is delayed. In the benchmark specification, where the active period is defined as 1974–1982, the strongest effect appears around one to two periods after the shock.
